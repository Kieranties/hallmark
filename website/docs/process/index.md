# The process

**Work advances by acts, and every act leaves something behind.**
[Capture](./capture.md) is open to anyone. Of the six acts that follow it, five are
[Worker](../parties/roles.md) acts — each advances the work and produces what the next act
needs. [Validation](./validate.md) is the one exception, held by the
[persona](../parties/personas.md) rather than the Worker, because its object is the outcome
and not the work.

| Act | Held by | Leaves behind |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Capture](./capture.md)** | Anyone | A request |
| **[Sift](./sift.md)** | Worker | A typed item, agreed to be done |
| **[Specify](./specify.md)** | Worker | Specifications that fail |
| **[Plan](./plan.md)** | Worker | An ordered route, and a declared touch |
| **[Action](./action.md)** | Worker | The change, and a result for every criterion |
| **[Validate](./validate.md)** | The persona, or a recorded stand-in | A judgement on the outcome |
| **[Publish](./publish.md)** | Worker | A delivered result, confirmed — and the state *complete* |

## Verification

**Verification is not one of these acts — it is the exit of each of them.** Specify, Plan and
Action all end with someone who did not do the work confirming what was produced. Validation
is the exception, and is a step of its own, because its object is different.

**Judging work against what was asked of it.** A [Verifier](../parties/roles.md) act, held by
someone who did not do the work, at the exit of every act that produces something — the
specifications, the plan, the change.

**Two outcomes only: it verifies, or it returns the work.** A verifier that cannot settle the
question from what is in front of it has found the evidence insufficient, which is a failed
verification and the Worker's to close. It does not escalate.

## What runs alongside

**[Commitment](../work/commitment.md) runs alongside these acts, never among them.** It is a
promise of *when* the work will arrive — made by a [Decider](../parties/roles.md), carried as
a property of the item rather than a stage it passes through, and changing nothing about the
acts or their order.

**Whether the work should still be done at all is not one of these acts either.** That is a
Decider question, and it may be asked at any point after the work was accepted: either the
work continues, or what was accepted is withdrawn and the item is `Retracted`.

*Where the work rests between one act and the next is written on [the states](./states.md).*
