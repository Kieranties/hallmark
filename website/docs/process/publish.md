# Publish

**Make it available — then go and look.** The result is put where the
[persona](../parties/personas.md) can reach it, and its arrival is then confirmed by querying
for it.

**Completion is not reported, it is found.** A report that something shipped is itself a
statement with no evidence behind it, and the practice does not accept one anywhere else
either. So the last act is to look: is the thing actually there?

**The [capability](../work/capability.md) is produced here, and produced automatically** —
generated from the [specifications](./specify.md) that passed, never authored by hand. What
the system is said to do therefore cannot drift from what was proven about it, because nobody
is in a position to write the first without the second.

**Complete is a state, not an act.** An item is complete because its publication was
confirmed — not because anyone marked it so. Nothing in the practice declares its own
completion.

## What `Complete` does not mean

**It does not mean anybody has it.** In much of the industry *generally available* means
customers have it. Read that way, this state reproduces exactly the failure the practice
exists to end — a claim that somebody has something, resting on the fact that it was
released. The closer analogue is *released to manufacturing*: it exists, it is retrievable,
and taking it is somebody else's act.

**It does not mean the work was right.** An item at `Complete` may still turn out to be wrong,
and finding that out raises a [fix](../work/fix.md) attached to the
[capability](../work/capability.md) it stated. Nothing is reopened: the track runs once per
item, and a correction is its own item travelling it again.

:::warning[If publishing routinely runs ahead of verification]
Verification becomes a formality nobody blocks on. **What is published-but-unverified has to
be distinguishable by whoever consumes it** — a prerelease, a separate channel, a tag. Without
that distinction the gate has no teeth, and what the system is said to do fills with things
nothing confirmed.
:::

> **Done when:** the published thing has been found by looking for it.
