# Action

**Do the work.** A [Worker](../parties/roles.md) act, like the [sift](./sift.md), the
[specification](./specify.md) and the [plan](./plan.md) before it — what differs is only what
it leaves behind.

**Every criterion is driven, not assumed.** The automated ones are run; the documented ones are
followed and the result recorded. A criterion nobody exercised has proved nothing, and a
specification that was never seen to pass is not evidence — it is an intention with a
filename.

What comes out is the change itself, and a result for every criterion that was set.

> **Done when:** every criterion has been driven, and holds.
>
> **Verified when:** someone who did not do the work confirms the change meets the
> specifications and the [standards](../work/standards.md), save for anything already
> [conceded](../apply/concessions.md).
