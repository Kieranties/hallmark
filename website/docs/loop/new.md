# New

## What it is \{#definition}

**An item is `New` when it is in [the door](../items/the-door.md) and nothing else has
happened to it.**

## Why it exists \{#why}

**The pile of unsifted work has to be visible, and this is what makes it so.** Everything
that arrives lands here and stays here until somebody looks at it. The count is therefore
the count of things nobody has yet ruled on — not an estimate of it, and not a number
anybody assembles.

**Without a first state that means nothing has happened, the pile hides.** Given somewhere
to put things that looks like progress — *triaged*, *acknowledged*, *under review* — the
backlog stops being countable, because the difference between *seen* and *ruled on*
disappears into a word that covers both.

**A state that means nothing has happened is also what makes capture free.** Whoever
writes something into the door incurs no obligation to shape it, because the first state
asks for nothing beyond its existence.

## Rules \{#rules}

**`New` is achieved when the item is in the door.** Nothing else is required and nothing
else is implied.

**An item arrives at `New` regardless of source or kind.**

**`New` says nothing about whether the item is real, whether it is a duplicate, or
whether it will be done.** All three are settled at the sift, which achieves
[`Accepted`](./accepted.md).

**An item may arrive carrying a [type](../items/types.md), and that type is not trusted
until the sift verifies it.**

**Nothing is rejected at `New`.** Ruling an item out is an act with a state of its own,
and it leaves a recorded reason behind — see [stopping](./stopping.md).

## In detail \{#detail}

### The count is the metric, and it is free

Nobody reports how much unsifted work exists. The items sitting at `New` **are** the
unsifted work, so the number is a query rather than a status update, and it cannot be
rounded down by anybody having a bad week.

A rising count is a fact about the rate of sifting against the rate of arrival. It is not
a fact about whoever is capturing, and using it that way reintroduces the cost at the
door that free capture exists to remove.

### What an item at `New` looks like

Four words. A half-formed hunch. A paste of an error nobody has read yet. That is the
expected shape of something at `New`, and none of it is a defect — a thin item is one the
sift will have to think about, which is the sift's job rather than the capturer's.
