# Working alone

## What it is \{#definition}

**Working alone is a repository with one [actor](../parties/actors.md), where the Verifier
role is unheld and that fact is recorded once rather than argued about per item.**

## Why it exists \{#why}

**One actor cannot both do the work and hold a fresh view of it.** The practice's core
invariant — the Verifier is not the Worker — cannot hold, and pretending otherwise
produces a green tick with nothing behind it.

**Recording it once is what keeps it visible without making it a daily argument.** An
unheld role written down at the start is a fact about the set-up. Re-litigated at every
item, it becomes a rule everybody has learned to route around, and the routing is what
stops being visible.

**Most of the defence survives one actor, and it is worth being precise about which
parts.** Writing what would make the work right *before* building it does not need a
second person. Driving every criterion does not need one. The one thing that does is the
fresh view, and saying so plainly is more useful than a practice that quietly assumes it.

## Rules \{#rules}

**Where there is one actor, the Verifier role is unheld, and that is recorded when the
repository is enabled.** It is not a rule being broken; it is a role nobody can hold.

**It is recorded once, not per item.**

**What still holds, and is not relaxed:**

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Acceptance criteria are written before the work exists** | That is the whole defence. You are checking against a target fixed in advance, not against what you remember wanting |
| **Every criterion is re-driven deliberately at [`Built`](./built.md)** | Not assumed from having just written the thing |
| **Both checks are run at [`Verified`](./verified.md)** | Conformance **and** currency, even though it is the same person doing it |

**A fresh agent session is the cheapest second actor there is.** Give it the item, the
criteria, the specification, the plan, the change and the standards.

**Do not give it the conversation in which the work was done.** That is the one thing a
verifier must not have, and handing it over turns an independent check into an expensive
echo. What is shared and what is withheld is on
[verification](./verification.md#rules).

**Getting a second actor is the largest single quality gain available**, and it is what
closes this out.

**Nothing on the [never conceded](../foundations/never-conceded.md) list is relaxed by
being alone.** Working alone changes who is available, not what is absolute.

## In detail \{#detail}

:::warning[The honest limit of working alone]
Even with a fresh actor, it has clean context and **you do not**. You know which part you
rushed, and you will steer it without meaning to — through the way the question is asked,
through what gets pasted in, through which answer gets accepted quickly.

This is why a second person is worth more than any tooling, and it is why the unheld role
stays recorded rather than being marked closed once an agent is in place.
:::

### Where escalation has nowhere to go

A Decider that cannot decide from the record normally hands the decision to another
Decider. With one actor there is nobody to hand it to, and there are two moves and no
third: the work stops, or the decision is made anyway and is a
[concession](./skipping-and-concessions.md). See
[escalation](../parties/escalation.md#detail).

### What working alone does not excuse

It does not excuse an item reaching `Built` with criteria nobody wrote, or reaching
`Verified` with only conformance checked. Both of those are steps passed over, and a step
passed over is a concession whether or not there was anybody else to do it. The count
stays honest, which is what makes the arrangement survivable rather than a slow erosion.
