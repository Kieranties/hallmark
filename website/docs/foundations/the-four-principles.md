# The four principles

## What it is \{#definition}

**Four principles — Provable, Derived, Traceable and Invariant — to one of which every
part of the practice traces, and anything tracing to none of them does not belong.**

## Why it exists \{#why}

**They are the test, not the slogan.** *Is this derived, or chosen?* has an answer. *Is
this provable, or asserted?* has an answer. *Would this change if the work were ten times
bigger, or if an agent did it?* has an answer.

**A principle you cannot fail is a slogan.** These can all be failed, which is what makes
them usable — and what makes it possible to say that a proposed mechanism does not
belong, on grounds somebody can check rather than on taste.

**They are used twice.** When *defining* the practice: does this decision serve a
principle? And when *applying* it: which principle is this choice targeting? A practice
whose principles are only read at the start is a practice with four decorative sentences
at the front.

## Rules \{#rules}

### 1 · Provable

> **Nothing is claimed that evidence does not prove.**

A claim may be stated only where a passing executable
[specification](../acts/required-outputs.md) proves it. What the system claims is
**generated** from what passed, never hand-authored. Before evidence can exist a claim is
**asserted** by an accountable party and marked as such; after it, the claim is derived
and nobody may assert it by hand.

### 2 · Derived

> **Everything computable is computed. Judgement is spent only where it must be.**

Rigour follows from what a change [touches](../loop/touch.md), never from an estimate.
The word for [stopping](../loop/stopping.md) follows from how far the work travelled.
[Slippage](../items/commitment.md) is the gap between the version promised and the
version delivered. The machine does everything mechanically possible and halts at the
exact point a consequence must be accepted rather than computed — and no earlier.

### 3 · Traceable

> **Every claim, state and decision points back to what produced it.**

One [door](../items/the-door.md), so nothing arrives unrecorded. Decisions are made from
recorded facts rather than from what somebody remembers. A compromise is not prevented —
it is [conceded](../loop/skipping-and-concessions.md), in writing, scoped and expiring
and counted.

### 4 · Invariant

> **The route does not change with the size of the work, or the kind of actor.**

A one-line change and a multi-year body of work travel the identical
[track](../loop/the-track.md); the apparatus scales with what the work touches rather
than with anybody's judgement of importance. Every role may be held by
[a human or an agent](../parties/actors.md), and the practice never presumes which.
**Headcount is not a lever.**

### The test

**Anything that traces to none of the four does not belong.** That is the disposal rule,
and it applies to mechanisms already in the practice as readily as to proposed ones.

## In detail \{#detail}

### How the practice traces to them

Each row names a mechanism and the page that owns it. The mechanisms are not restated
here.

| Mechanism | Principle |
| --- | --- |
| A claim is claimable only where a passing [specification](../acts/required-outputs.md) proves it | **Provable** |
| [`Completed`](../loop/completed.md) means published *and proven* published — queried, not reported | **Provable** |
| [Acceptance criteria](../loop/specified.md) written before the work exists | **Provable · Traceable** |
| Rigour derived from [touch](../loop/touch.md) | **Derived** |
| The [stopping](../loop/stopping.md) word derived from state and commitment | **Derived** |
| [Slippage](../items/commitment.md) computed from target version against actual | **Derived** |
| Which [enforcement route](../acts/the-standards-stack.md) a standard takes, derived rather than chosen | **Derived** |
| One [door](../items/the-door.md) — every signal in one queue | **Traceable** |
| A [Decider](../parties/the-four-roles.md) decides from recorded facts, never from session memory | **Traceable** |
| The [concession](../loop/skipping-and-concessions.md) — a compromise recorded, scoped, expiring, counted | **Traceable** |
| The same [track](../loop/the-track.md) for a typo and a programme | **Invariant** |
| Any role held by [a human or an agent](../parties/actors.md) | **Invariant** |
| An unheld role is [recorded](../loop/working-alone.md), not worked around silently | **Traceable · Invariant** |

Several more mechanisms trace to these principles and belong to sections not yet
written — what the system claims being generated rather than authored, and a claim
leaving only by a recorded removal, both trace to **Provable**.

:::important[The test is falsifiable, and that is the point]
Each of the four states something that could be otherwise, and each can be checked
against a specific decision. A practice whose principles cannot be failed has four
sentences that approve of everything, including the next mechanism nobody should have
built.
:::

### Where two principles pull against each other

They do, occasionally, and the practice records where rather than pretending otherwise.
**Traceable** asks that everything point back to what produced it, and the pages here
state facts without provenance — no account of what a definition used to say, and no
history inside a definition. The trade is deliberate: provenance belongs to items
travelling the track, not to the practice's own prose.
