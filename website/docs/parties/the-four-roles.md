# The four roles

## What it is \{#definition}

**A role is the position an [actor](./actors.md) holds on a subject, and there are four:
Worker, Verifier, Sentinel and Decider.**

## Why it exists \{#why}

**All four are defined by their relationship to context.** The Worker accumulates it;
the other three are denied it. That is not four separate rules — it is one principle,
which used to apply only to checking work and turns out to organise the whole set.

**Role is an interface; the actor is an implementation.** Two actors holding the same
role are interchangeable, because everything the position needs sits on the position.
Without that, delegating a role would mean re-granting a bundle of things nobody has
listed.

**Four is the whole set on purpose.** A short list is learnable by a team with no
practice; a long one is skimmed. Every act on or with the system is one of advancing it,
confirming it, watching it, or committing to it.

## Rules \{#rules}

### The four

| Role | Does | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Worker** | Performs the required action on the work at that time — researching, coding, drafting | **Accumulates.** The only role that does |
| **Verifier** | Confirms the Worker's work against a standard, so a fresh view is always used | **Denied** — never saw the doing |
| **Sentinel** | Watches, always on, for drift, staleness, cost, vulnerability and conditions becoming favourable | **Outside** any work session, by construction |
| **Decider** | Commits money, risk, or a promise to a client | **Denied** — decides from the record |

**One verb each: Worker advances · Verifier confirms · Sentinel watches · Decider
commits.**

### What binds each of them

**The Verifier is not the Worker.** This is
[never conceded](../foundations/never-conceded.md). There is no item, no deadline and no
shortage of actors that permits one actor to hold both roles on one item.

**A Verifier has exactly two outcomes: it confirms, or it sends the work back**, and it
does not [escalate](./escalation.md). What the act itself requires is on
[verification](../loop/verification.md).

**A Sentinel's loop period is a declared property.** What a standing watch can promise
depends on how often it runs, so an undeclared period is a watch nobody can rely on.

**A Decider decides from recorded facts, never from the working session.** That is what
makes a decision reconstructable afterwards.

**Any role may be held by either kind of actor, including Decider.** Which kind holds it
is never part of the rule.

**One actor may hold every role** — subject to the one exception above, which binds per
item rather than per repository.

**A role is scoped to a subject, and carries three things.** See
[what a role carries](./what-a-role-carries.md).

## In detail \{#detail}

### The roles are not delivery positions

Because the [structure](./the-structure.md) has two branches, the four roles have to
describe any action on or with the system. An Operator restarting a service is a
**Worker**. An Auditor reviewing logs is a **Verifier**. Reading them as stations on the
track is the common mistake, and it is what makes the persona branch look decorative.

### A role name never implies who holds it

Each name was chosen against one criterion: it must not imply which kind of actor holds
it. *Reviewer* implies a person reading; *approver* implies a signature; *owner* implies
a job. Worker, Verifier, Sentinel and Decider each name what is done, and leave who does
it to eligibility.

:::important[The price of a Decider that decides only from the record]
It makes **recording mandatory before every decision.** A human in the room today
decides partly on things they know and never wrote down; this Decider cannot. That moves
the artifacts nobody has a home for from *a later pass* to *a precondition of the role
model working at all*.
:::
