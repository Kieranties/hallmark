# Actors

**A human or an agent**, and the practice never presumes which. An actor may hold a
[role](./roles.md) if — and only if — it has what that role requires: **eligibility turns on
access, not on actor kind.** There is no presumption that a human is the better actor, or the
default one.

**Every actor must be *distinctly identifiable*** — a username, an email, a session id.
Identity is not bureaucracy; it is what makes [accountability](./accountability.md)
traceable.

The consequence is that the practice runs all-human, all-agent, or any mix, without
alteration. It is also why reachability of information is not a later concern — it decides who
is eligible to act at all.
