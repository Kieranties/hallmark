# The door

## What it is \{#definition}

**The door is the one place every signal about the work lands, whatever its source and
whatever its kind.**

## Why it exists \{#why}

**A second queue guarantees internal work starves.** Given somewhere for client requests
and somewhere else for chores, upkeep, security findings and debt, the second list loses
every time — not by decision, but by never being the loudest thing in the room. One
queue is the only arrangement in which internal work competes fairly.

**Charge for capture and the ideas go back into people's heads.** A quality bar at the
entrance looks like a filter on noise and works as a filter on effort: whoever has to
write four fields for a hunch keeps the hunch. What is kept is invisible, and invisible
work is the thing the practice is built to end.

**Signals arrive everywhere, so somewhere has to be the record.** An error report, a
conversation, an email, a thought at two in the morning — the arrival point is not the
door. Whoever receives one writes it into the door, and after that the practice can say
what exists.

## Rules \{#rules}

### One door

**Every signal enters the same queue, regardless of source or kind.** A client request,
a defect, a chore, a security finding, a question, a standing watch firing, and debt
already owed all arrive in one place.

**No second queue is made for internal work.**

**Whoever receives a signal writes it into the door.** The arrival point does not matter;
the record does.

### Items

**An item is anything that enters the door**, and everything in the door is an item.

**Every item carries exactly one [type](./types.md)**, which the sift assigns or verifies.

**An item enters at [`New`](../loop/new.md)**, the first position on
[the track](../loop/the-track.md).

### Capture

**Capture is free.** No ceremony, no quality bar, no minimum detail, no approval, and no
penalty for raising something that turns out to be nothing.

**Free means no ceremony — it does not mean no fields.** A tracker asking for a title is
not ceremony.

**Capture is open to anyone**, including a party the system serves. Capturing is not
acting on the change, so nobody becomes a [discipline](../parties/disciplines.md) by
doing it.

**Nothing is rejected at capture.** Whether something is real, a duplicate, or not to be
done is settled at the sift, which is an act with a state of its own.

## In detail \{#detail}

### The door is one place, not one shape

A door is whatever the repository already has that can hold items and be queried — an
issue tracker, at the size most repositories start at. What matters is that there is
exactly one, that it can be pointed at, and that everything is in it. How it carries an
item's type, state, commitment and markers is an application concern.

### What free capture buys, and what it does not

It buys the count. Everything raised is visible, including the things that turn out to
be nothing, and the pile of unsifted items is a fact about the practice rather than a
number somebody assembles. What it does not buy is quality: most of what arrives is thin,
and thin is the expected shape of a capture rather than a defect in it.

The sift is where thin becomes typed and ruled on, and the count of items waiting for it
is deliberately impossible to hide — see [`New`](../loop/new.md).

### Finding work is a separate signal

The door holds everything; it does not say what to pick up. That is a
[marker](./markers-and-claiming.md), and keeping the two apart is what stops the state
field quietly becoming a priority queue.
