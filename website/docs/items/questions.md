# Questions

## What it is \{#definition}

**A question is an item asking for information, and one of the two
[types](./types.md) that do not travel the track.**

## Why it exists \{#why}

**A question is a probe of the knowledge substrate.** Somebody needed something written
down and went looking for it. Whether they found it, and where it turned out to live, is
information about the substrate that arrives for free every time somebody asks.

**How it resolves is diagnostic, and the three findings have three different remedies.**
Treating them as one — *somebody asked, somebody answered, close it* — throws away the
only signal in the exchange, and the practice ends up with a well-answered set of
questions and no idea why they keep arriving.

**The rate of questions arriving is a signal about the substrate, not about the people
asking.** Read the other way it becomes a reason to discourage asking, which raises the
count of things nobody knows while lowering the count of things anybody said.

## Rules \{#rules}

**A question is a request for information.**

**A question runs `New → Accepted → Answered → Closed`** and does not travel
[the track](../loop/the-track.md). Nothing is promised and nothing is built.

**A question is never [committed](./commitment.md) to a version.**

**`Answered` and `Closed` are two acts, not one.** `Answered` is the act of answering.
`Closed` is accepting that the answer was sufficient — which is a judgement, and can be
withheld.

**Every question resolves to exactly one of three findings, and each raises something
different.**

| The answer | The finding | What it raises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existed and was findable | **Retrieval is failing.** The question should not have reached the door | Something about how the substrate is found, not about the answer |
| Existed only in somebody's head | It has to be written down | A chore, or a capability |
| Did not exist | Something is **undesigned** | A decision to take, not work to do |

**A question that raises another item does not become that item.** It is answered and
closed on its own terms; what it surfaced enters [the door](./the-door.md) as its own
item.

## In detail \{#detail}

### What a question does not travel, and why that is the point

It does not travel the track because the track is a route to something being claimed,
and a question claims nothing. There is no outcome to state, no specification to fail
first, nothing to build and nothing to publish. Running one through those states would
produce five empty achievements and a false position on the board.

The other type off the track is the service request — the same shape, pointed at an
action rather than at information.

### The rate is the measurement, not the individual question

One question tells you about one thing somebody could not find. A month of them tells
you which part of the substrate is not reachable, and that is the reading worth acting
on. A rising rate is a finding about what is written down and how it is found; it is
never a finding about who is asking.

:::note[Closing is an acceptance]
`Closed` is the asker accepting the answer was sufficient, which means it can be refused
or left unanswered. An answer nobody closes is an answer that did not land, and that is
worth noticing rather than tidying away.
:::
