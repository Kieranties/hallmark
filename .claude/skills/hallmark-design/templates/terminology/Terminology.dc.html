Terminology

Every word the practice defines, with the branch or act it belongs to. Where a bare name could be read two ways, it is qualified until it can only be one.

Actor

Party

A human or an agent — the thing that actually holds a role. Every actor must be distinctly identifiable, because identity is what makes accountability traceable.

See also Role, Delegation

Concession

Record

To concede is to state that a standard has not been met, name who accepted that, and proceed anyway. A concession is not an exception to the practice — it is the practice working.

See also Standard

Sift

Act

Ruling on a request — the first act on the change, and the first that requires a role. Two things settle: what type the request carries, and whether it will be done at all.

See also Request, Work types

Standard

Standard

A statement of what must hold, expressed as a floor. Standards stack by union: a layer may add or constrain, never contradict. One that cannot be met is conceded, never quietly dropped.

See also Concession
