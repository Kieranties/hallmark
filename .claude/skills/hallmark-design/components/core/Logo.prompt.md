Brand lockup — use in navbars, footers, hero units and anywhere the practice is identified.

```jsx
<Logo size={28} />
<Logo size={20} wordmark={false} />
<Logo size={40} tone="inverse" />
```

The initial inside the stamp is derived from `label`. Mark alone (`wordmark={false}`) is the favicon/avatar form. Never re-colour the mark outside the gold, ink and inverse tones, and never place it on a mid-tone photograph without a solid plate behind it.
